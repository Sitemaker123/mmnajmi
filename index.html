ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Biography

Mahmood Mian Najmi is a distinguished author, Senior Lawyer, and religious scholar known for his research-based yet deeply touching writing style.

His literature focuses on Seerat-un-Nabi (SAW), Islamic history, the history of Makkah and Madinah, and the lives of the Sahaba and Sahabiyaat. Written with simplicity and authenticity, his works aim to bridge historical scholarship with heart-felt spiritual guidance for contemporary readers.

His scholarly articles are regularly featured in Daily Jang and other leading publications.
